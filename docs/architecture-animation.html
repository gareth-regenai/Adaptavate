The Calcs sheet is the whole point of this architecture. It is where Adaptavate's real formulas live, it runs only on the server, and nothing in it is ever included in the response. The browser receives twelve finished numbers and has no way to work backwards to the model that produced them. Timings in the animation are slowed for legibility, the real round trip measured 16ms.
